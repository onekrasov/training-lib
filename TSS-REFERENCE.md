# Training Stress Score (TSS) Reference

## What is TSS?

Training Stress Score (TSS) quantifies the training load of a workout. It considers both intensity and duration, giving you a single number to understand workout difficulty and plan weekly training load.

## TSS Guidelines

### Daily TSS Targets
- **Recovery day**: 0-40 TSS
- **Easy day**: 40-70 TSS
- **Moderate day**: 70-120 TSS
- **Hard day**: 120-180 TSS
- **Very hard day**: 180-250+ TSS

### Weekly TSS Targets (Total across all activities)
- **Beginner**: 200-400 TSS/week
- **Intermediate**: 400-600 TSS/week
- **Advanced amateur**: 600-800 TSS/week
- **Competitive**: 800-1000+ TSS/week

## TSS Accumulation Rules

### Daily
- Don't exceed 2-3 hard workouts (>120 TSS) per week
- Allow 48 hours between hard sessions
- Easy days should stay <70 TSS

### Weekly
- Don't increase weekly TSS by more than 5-10% week-to-week
- Every 3-4 weeks, take a recovery week (reduce TSS by 30-40%)
- Monitor 7-day rolling TSS to avoid overtraining

### Recovery Guidelines
- After 150+ TSS workout: need 36-48 hours recovery
- After 200+ TSS workout: need 48-72 hours recovery
- Listen to your body - HRV and resting heart rate are key indicators

## Fitness Metrics

### CTL (Chronic Training Load)
- Rolling 42-day average of daily TSS
- Represents your fitness level
- Target: Slowly build CTL over weeks/months

### ATL (Acute Training Load)
- Rolling 7-day average of daily TSS
- Represents your fatigue level

### TSB (Training Stress Balance)
- Formula: CTL - ATL
- Negative = fatigued, Positive = fresh
- Race day target: +10 to +25 TSB

## Coach's Notes

**File naming helps you:**
1. **Plan weekly load**: Add up TSS to hit your weekly target
2. **Sequence workouts**: Balance hard and easy days
3. **Track fitness**: Monitor if you're progressing appropriately
4. **Avoid overtraining**: Don't stack too many high-TSS days

**Example:** If you're targeting 600 TSS/week, you might do:
- 1x 200 TSS ride (long weekend ride)
- 2x 120-165 TSS workouts (hard sessions)
- 2x 60-80 TSS workouts (moderate sessions)
- 2x 30-40 TSS or rest (recovery)

Total: ~600-650 TSS balanced across the week
