# Training Guide for Amateur Athletes

## Understanding Workout Intensities

All workouts use percentage-based zones relative to your FTP (Functional Threshold Power for cycling) or threshold pace (for running/swimming):

### Cycling & Running Zones:
- **50-60%**: Active recovery, very easy
- **60-70%**: Easy aerobic, conversational pace
- **70-80%**: Moderate aerobic, endurance pace
- **80-88%**: Tempo, comfortably hard
- **88-95%**: Sweetspot/Threshold, hard but sustainable
- **95-105%**: Threshold, race pace for 40km-60km
- **105-120%**: VO2max, very hard, short intervals
- **120%+**: Anaerobic, sprint efforts

### Swimming Zones:
- **50-60%**: Recovery swimming, technique focus
- **60-75%**: Aerobic endurance
- **75-85%**: Tempo/moderate effort
- **85-95%**: Threshold, race pace
- **95-110%**: Above threshold, hard intervals

## How to Use This Library

### 1. Choose Your Event
Start with the event plan that matches your goal:
- `events/mallorca-312.md` - Long distance cycling
- `events/olympic-triathlon.md` - Multi-sport endurance

### 2. Follow the Periodization
Each plan follows proven periodization:
- **Base**: Build aerobic foundation (60-70% of time in easy zones)
- **Build**: Add intensity and volume (70-80% threshold work)
- **Peak**: Race-specific efforts at maximum volume
- **Taper**: Reduce volume 40-50%, maintain intensity

### 3. Adapt to Your Life
**Key Principles:**
- Consistency beats intensity - better to do 80% of workouts at 90% than 50% at 100%
- If you miss a workout, skip it and continue the plan
- If fatigued, replace hard workout with easy/recovery
- Life happens - adjust but maintain pattern of hard/easy days

### 4. Recovery is Training
- Sleep 7-9 hours per night
- Eat enough - don't diet during hard training blocks
- Easy days should feel EASY (conversational pace)
- Take complete rest days when prescribed

## Training Tips

### Nutrition
**During Training:**
- Rides/runs <90min: Water only
- Rides/runs >90min: 60-90g carbs/hour
- Swims: Hydrate between sets

**Daily:**
- Eat enough protein (1.6g per kg bodyweight)
- Don't restrict carbs during hard training blocks
- Focus on whole foods

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. **Going too hard on easy days** - Most common mistake. Easy means EASY.
2. **Not going hard enough on hard days** - When it says 95%, don't do 85%
3. **Skipping recovery weeks** - Your body needs consolidation time
4. **Overtraining** - More is not always better. Follow the plan.
5. **Poor pacing** - Starting workouts or races too fast

### When to Adjust
**Skip or reduce if:**
- Resting heart rate is 10+ bpm elevated
- Persistent fatigue for 2+ days
- Feeling run down or getting sick
- Motivation is completely gone

**Add easy volume if:**
- Recovering well from all workouts
- Feeling strong and motivated
- Not time-constrained

### Testing & Benchmarks
**Every 6-8 weeks, test your fitness:**
- **Cycling**: 20min FTP test or ramp test
- **Running**: 5km time trial or 20min threshold test
- **Swimming**: 1000m time trial

Update your training zones based on new numbers.

## Questions?
