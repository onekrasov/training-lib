# Mallorca 312 Training Plan

**Event:** Mallorca 312 (312km cycling with ~5000m elevation)
**Duration:** 16 weeks
**Target:** Complete the event comfortably in 10-12 hours
**Weekly Volume:** 10-20 hours (progressive build)

## Training Phases

### Base Phase (Weeks 1-6)
Build aerobic endurance and climbing strength foundation.

**Week Structure:**
- Monday: Rest or [75-technique-drills](../sports/swim/75-technique-drills.icu) (active recovery swim)
- Tuesday: [88-sweetspot-2x20](../sports/bike/88-sweetspot-2x20.icu)
- Wednesday: [32-recovery-ride](../sports/bike/32-recovery-ride.icu)
- Thursday: [115-climbing-repeats](../sports/bike/115-climbing-repeats.icu)
- Friday: Rest
- Saturday: [165-long-endurance-4h](../sports/bike/165-long-endurance-4h.icu) (progress to [205-long-endurance-5h](../sports/bike/205-long-endurance-5h.icu) by week 6)
- Sunday: [125-endurance-3h](../sports/bike/125-endurance-3h.icu) OR [145-endurance-3000m](../sports/swim/145-endurance-3000m.icu) (cross-training)

**Weekly Volume:** 10-14 hours
**Key Focus:** Build aerobic base, introduce climbing work

### Build Phase (Weeks 7-12)
Increase volume and intensity. Add race-specific efforts.

**Week Structure:**
- Monday: Rest or [75-technique-drills](../sports/swim/75-technique-drills.icu) (active recovery swim)
- Tuesday: [82-over-unders](../sports/bike/82-over-unders.icu) OR [72-threshold-intervals](../sports/bike/72-threshold-intervals.icu)
- Wednesday: [32-recovery-ride](../sports/bike/32-recovery-ride.icu)
- Thursday: [78-vo2max-intervals](../sports/bike/78-vo2max-intervals.icu)
- Friday: Rest or [145-endurance-3000m](../sports/swim/145-endurance-3000m.icu) (easy swim)
- Saturday: [205-long-endurance-5h](../sports/bike/205-long-endurance-5h.icu) with climbing
- Sunday: [125-endurance-3h](../sports/bike/125-endurance-3h.icu)

**Weekly Volume:** 14-18 hours
**Key Focus:** Build specific endurance, maintain intensity

### Peak Phase (Weeks 13-14)
Maximum volume with race-specific efforts.

**Week Structure:**
- Monday: Rest or [75-technique-drills](../sports/swim/75-technique-drills.icu) (active recovery swim)
- Tuesday: [165-race-simulation](../sports/bike/165-race-simulation.icu)
- Wednesday: [32-recovery-ride](../sports/bike/32-recovery-ride.icu)
- Thursday: [115-climbing-repeats](../sports/bike/115-climbing-repeats.icu)
- Friday: Rest
- Saturday: 6-hour ride at race pace (70-75% FTP)
- Sunday: 2-3 hour easy ride

**Weekly Volume:** 18-20 hours
**Key Focus:** Race simulation, build confidence

### Taper Phase (Weeks 15-16)
Reduce volume, maintain intensity, rest and recover.

**Week 15:**
- Monday: Rest or [75-technique-drills](../sports/swim/75-technique-drills.icu) (active recovery)
- Tuesday: [82-over-unders](../sports/bike/82-over-unders.icu)
- Wednesday: [32-recovery-ride](../sports/bike/32-recovery-ride.icu)
- Thursday: [115-climbing-repeats](../sports/bike/115-climbing-repeats.icu) (reduced volume)
- Friday: Rest
- Saturday: 3-hour steady ride
- Sunday: 90min easy ride

**Week 16 (Race Week):**
- Monday: Rest
- Tuesday: 60min easy with 3x5min at race pace
- Wednesday: Rest or 45min easy spin
- Thursday: Rest
- Friday: 30min easy spin + race prep
- Saturday: **RACE DAY**
- Sunday: Easy recovery spin

## Coaching Notes

**Nutrition:** Practice your race nutrition on all rides >3 hours. Aim for 60-90g carbs/hour.

**Climbing:** Mallorca has significant climbs (Puig Major, etc.). Do climbing-specific work on hills when possible.

**Pacing:** Learn your sustainable pace. The first 100km should feel easy. Many riders blow up at 200km from going too hard early.

**Bike Fit:** Get a professional fit. 312km will expose any position issues.

**Group Riding:** Practice riding in groups if you plan to draft during the event.

**Recovery:** Recovery weeks (every 3-4 weeks) reduce volume by 30-40% to absorb training load.

**Swimming for Recovery:** Swimming 1-2x per week provides excellent active recovery for cyclists:
- Low-impact recovery aids muscle repair
- Different movement patterns reduce cycling-specific fatigue
- Maintains cardiovascular fitness without bike/leg stress
- Improves flexibility and core strength
- Technique-focused swims keep intensity low
- Schedule swimming on easy days (Monday) or as alternative to second bike ride (Sunday/Friday)
