# Oceanman 5K Training Plan

**Event:** Ibiza Oceanman 5K Open Water Swim
**Distance:** 5,000 meters (5K)
**Duration:** 8 weeks
**Target:** Complete comfortably in 90-150 minutes
**Weekly Volume:** 4-8km swimming (1-2 sessions per week)

## Overview

This is a **minimalist plan** designed for amateur swimmers who want to complete a 5K open water swim without overtraining. With just 1-2 swims per week (4-8km total), you can build the fitness and skills needed to finish comfortably.

**Prerequisites:**
- Can swim 2km continuously without stopping
- Basic freestyle technique
- Some pool swimming experience

## Training Phases

### Base Phase (Weeks 1-3)
Build endurance to 3-4km continuous swimming.

**Week Structure (2 swims per week):**
- **Swim 1:** [145-endurance-3000m](../sports/swim/145-endurance-3000m.icu) OR 2500m easy continuous
- **Swim 2:** [75-technique-drills](../sports/swim/75-technique-drills.icu) OR 2000m with drills

**Alternative (1 swim per week):**
- **Weekend:** 3000-3500m easy swim, mix of continuous and intervals

**Weekly Volume:** 4-5km
**Key Focus:** Build to 3km continuous, improve technique, stay consistent

### Build Phase (Weeks 4-6)
Increase distance and add open water practice.

**Week Structure (2 swims per week):**
- **Swim 1:** [185-endurance-4000m](../sports/swim/185-endurance-4000m.icu) OR 3500m easy continuous
- **Swim 2:** Open water practice 2-3km OR [145-endurance-3000m](../sports/swim/145-endurance-3000m.icu)

**Alternative (1 swim per week):**
- **Weekend:** 4000-4500m steady swim OR 3-4km open water

**Weekly Volume:** 5-7km
**Key Focus:** Build to 4km continuous, practice open water swimming, sighting skills

### Peak Phase (Weeks 7)
Complete the full distance in training.

**Week Structure:**
- **Swim 1:** [225-endurance-5000m](../sports/swim/225-endurance-5000m.icu) OR 5km continuous easy
- **Swim 2:** Easy 2000m technique OR open water practice 2-3km

**Alternative (1 swim per week):**
- **Weekend:** 5km continuous swim (race simulation)

**Weekly Volume:** 5-7km
**Key Focus:** Complete 5km distance once, build confidence, practice pacing

### Taper (Week 8)
Rest and prepare.

**Race Week:**
- **Early week:** Easy 2000-2500m with some race pace efforts
- **Mid-week:** Easy 1500m technique focus OR rest
- **Friday:** Rest or very easy 1000m
- **Saturday:** **RACE DAY**

**Weekly Volume:** 2-4km
**Key Focus:** Stay fresh, maintain feel, mental preparation

## Minimal Training Approach

### Why This Works
**Less is More:**
- 5K open water is 80% technique and pacing, 20% fitness
- Consistency beats volume for amateur swimmers
- 1-2 swims per week prevents burnout and injury
- Your body adapts efficiently with proper recovery

**Keys to Success:**
- Don't skip weeks - consistency is everything
- Focus on completing distance, not speed
- Practice open water skills (sighting, navigation)
- One long swim per week is sufficient

### Open Water Skills (Critical!)

**Sighting (Practice Every Swim):**
- Lift eyes every 6-10 strokes
- Don't lift entire head, just eyes above water
- Time with breathing stroke
- Use distant landmarks, not buoys

**Navigation:**
- Swim straight using shore landmarks
- Practice in pool: swim with eyes closed 10 strokes
- Draft behind other swimmers to save energy
- Wide turns around buoys are easier

**Wetsuit:**
- Rent or borrow for 2-3 practice swims minimum
- Apply bodyglide/vaseline at neck, arms, ankles
- Wetsuit adds 5-10% speed
- Practice getting it off quickly

**Open Water Practice:**
- Minimum 2-3 open water swims before race
- Practice in similar conditions if possible
- Get comfortable with waves, chop, murky water
- Find a swimming buddy for safety

## Nutrition

### During Race (90+ minutes)
**Optional but recommended:**
- Take gel/drink at feeding station if offered
- 30-45 minute intervals
- Practice treading water and drinking in training
- Stay hydrated (dehydration = slow times)

### Race Day
- Light breakfast 2-3 hours before (banana, toast, energy bar)
- Hydrate steadily until 30 min before
- Nothing new or experimental
- Post-race: protein + carbs within 30 minutes

## Race Day Strategy

### Pre-Race (2-3 hours before)
- Light breakfast 2-3 hours before
- Arrive early, check course and landmarks
- Warm up 10-15 minutes before start
- Stay calm, trust your training

### Pacing Strategy
**The Golden Rule: Start SLOW**
- First 500m feels chaotic - stay calm
- First 1km should feel EASY (you'll pass people later)
- Most people go out too fast and suffer
- Settle into sustainable rhythm by 1km

**During the Swim:**
- Break into 1km segments mentally
- Count strokes to stay engaged (I do 50 per arm = ~100m)
- Sight every 6-10 strokes
- Focus on smooth, long strokes
- Take feed at 45-60 min if offered

**Final Push:**
- Last 500m: pick up effort gradually
- Final 200m: sprint to finish
- Stay focused on navigation to the end

### Common Amateur Mistakes
1. **Starting too fast** (ruins your race!)
2. **Poor sighting** (swimming extra distance)
3. **Panicking in chaos** (stay calm, breathe)
4. **Not practicing open water** (do at least 2-3 OW swims!)
5. **New equipment on race day** (test wetsuit first!)

### Post-Race
- Easy walk/swim for 5-10 minutes
- Eat and drink within 30 minutes
- Take your time exiting water (avoid dizziness)
- Celebrate - you did it!

## Coach's Real Talk

### What You Actually Need
**Minimum to Complete 5K Comfortably:**
- Can swim 3km continuously in pool
- 2-3 open water practice swims
- Basic sighting skills
- Consistent training (don't skip weeks)
- One 5km swim completed in training

**What You DON'T Need:**
- Perfect technique (good enough is fine)
- Speed work or intervals
- 6+ days per week training
- Expensive coaching
- Racing experience

### Time Expectations (Realistic)
- **Beginner swimmer:** 2:00-2:30 (comfortable pace)
- **Average amateur:** 1:30-2:00 (steady effort)
- **Experienced swimmer:** 1:15-1:30 (strong pace)

**Remember:** Finishing is the goal, not speed. Pool pace + 10-15% = open water pace typically.

### Mental Game
**Break It Down:**
- 5km = 5 x 1km segments or 50 x 100m
- Count strokes to pass time
- Have a mantra: "smooth and steady"
- When it gets hard (it will), focus on next buoy

**It's Normal To:**
- Feel anxious before the race
- Struggle with sighting at first
- Get tired around 3km mark
- Question why you're doing this (around 4km!)
- Feel amazing at the finish

### Biggest Success Factors
1. **Consistency** - Don't skip training weeks
2. **Patience** - Start the race SLOW
3. **Open water practice** - Do at least 2-3 swims
4. **Sighting** - Practice every training swim
5. **Confidence** - Complete 5km once in training

## Equipment

**Essential:**
- Wetsuit (rent/borrow if possible)
- 2 pairs of goggles (clear + tinted)
- Bodyglide/vaseline for chafing
- Bright swim cap (usually provided)

**Nice to Have:**
- GPS swim watch (track distance/pace)
- Pull buoy and fins (training tools)
- Anti-fog spray

**Race Day Bag:**
- Towel and warm clothes
- Post-race snack and drink
- Sunscreen
- Plastic bag for wet gear
- ID and race bib
