# Olympic Triathlon Training Plan

**Event:** Olympic Distance Triathlon (1.5km swim, 40km bike, 10km run)
**Duration:** 12 weeks
**Target:** Complete sub 3 hours (amateur goal)
**Weekly Volume:** 8-14 hours (progressive build)

## Training Phases

### Base Phase (Weeks 1-4)
Build aerobic foundation across all three sports.

**Week Structure:**
- Monday: Rest or [75-technique-drills](../sports/swim/75-technique-drills.icu)
- Tuesday: [88-sweetspot-2x20](../sports/bike/88-sweetspot-2x20.icu) + [42-brick-run-30m](../sports/run/42-brick-run-30m.icu)
- Wednesday: [145-endurance-3000m](../sports/swim/145-endurance-3000m.icu)
- Thursday: [48-tempo-run](../sports/run/48-tempo-run.icu)
- Friday: [125-threshold-intervals](../sports/swim/125-threshold-intervals.icu) OR Rest
- Saturday: [165-long-endurance-4h](../sports/bike/165-long-endurance-4h.icu)
- Sunday: [88-long-run-90m](../sports/run/88-long-run-90m.icu)

**Weekly Volume:** 8-10 hours
**Key Focus:** Build aerobic base, practice transitions

### Build Phase (Weeks 5-9)
Increase intensity and race-specific work.

**Week Structure:**
- Monday: [75-technique-drills](../sports/swim/75-technique-drills.icu)
- Tuesday: [82-over-unders](../sports/bike/82-over-unders.icu) + [42-brick-run-30m](../sports/run/42-brick-run-30m.icu)
- Wednesday: [135-race-pace-1500m](../sports/swim/135-race-pace-1500m.icu)
- Thursday: [62-threshold-intervals-5x1k](../sports/run/62-threshold-intervals-5x1k.icu)
- Friday: [105-speed-intervals](../sports/swim/105-speed-intervals.icu) OR Rest
- Saturday: [165-race-simulation](../sports/bike/165-race-simulation.icu) (2.5 hours)
- Sunday: [68-race-pace-intervals](../sports/run/68-race-pace-intervals.icu)

**Weekly Volume:** 10-12 hours
**Key Focus:** Build race pace fitness, brick workouts

### Peak Phase (Weeks 10-11)
Race-specific intensity with full-distance workouts.

**Week 10:**
- Monday: [75-technique-drills](../sports/swim/75-technique-drills.icu)
- Tuesday: [72-threshold-intervals](../sports/bike/72-threshold-intervals.icu) + [42-brick-run-30m](../sports/run/42-brick-run-30m.icu)
- Wednesday: [135-race-pace-1500m](../sports/swim/135-race-pace-1500m.icu)
- Thursday: [68-vo2max-intervals-6x800](../sports/run/68-vo2max-intervals-6x800.icu)
- Friday: Rest
- Saturday: Olympic distance race simulation
- Sunday: [42-easy-run-45m](../sports/run/42-easy-run-45m.icu)

**Week 11:**
- Monday: [145-endurance-3000m](../sports/swim/145-endurance-3000m.icu)
- Tuesday: [60-norwegian-4x4](../sports/bike/60-norwegian-4x4.icu) + [42-brick-run-30m](../sports/run/42-brick-run-30m.icu)
- Wednesday: [125-threshold-intervals](../sports/swim/125-threshold-intervals.icu)
- Thursday: [48-tempo-run](../sports/run/48-tempo-run.icu)
- Friday: Rest
- Saturday: 2-hour bike + 45min run brick
- Sunday: Easy 60min run

**Weekly Volume:** 12-14 hours
**Key Focus:** Race simulation, build confidence

### Taper Phase (Week 12)
Reduce volume, maintain sharpness, rest.

**Race Week:**
- Monday: 20min easy swim with some pace efforts
- Tuesday: 60min bike with 3x5min at race pace
- Wednesday: 30min run with 5x1min at race pace
- Thursday: 20min easy swim OR Rest
- Friday: Rest or very easy 20min spin
- Saturday: **RACE DAY**
- Sunday: Easy recovery swim

## Coaching Notes

**Swim:**
- Focus on technique first, speed second
- Practice sighting and open water skills if possible
- Swim with wetsuit in training to get comfortable

**Bike:**
- Practice race pace (zone 3/threshold)
- Aero position work for time savings
- Nutrition: 40-60g carbs/hour on bike

**Run:**
- Brick runs are crucial - practice running off the bike tired
- Race pace should feel "comfortably hard"
- Practice race nutrition

**Transitions:**
- Practice T1 (swim-to-bike) and T2 (bike-to-run)
- Set up your transition area efficiently
- Quick transitions can save 2-3 minutes

**Pacing:**
- Swim: Start controlled, don't burn matches in the chaos
- Bike: Aim for negative split or even effort
- Run: First 2km at control, then settle into race pace

**Race Week:**
- No hard training after Tuesday
- Focus on sleep, nutrition, hydration
- Check all equipment by Wednesday

**Recovery:**
- Include recovery weeks (every 3-4 weeks) with 30% less volume
- Listen to your body - better to undertrain slightly than overtrain
